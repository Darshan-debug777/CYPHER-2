## YOUR CONTEXTUAL ANALYSIS TASK

Analyze the event data systematically:

1. **User Identity & Privileges**: Is the account used in expected ways? Are privilege escalation or unusual roles observed?
2. **Device & Host**: Is the host appropriate for the activity (workstation vs production server vs domain controller)?
3. **Network & Location**: Are source IPs, geographical locations, or VPN connections expected or anomalous?
4. **Temporal Patterns**: Are timestamps aligned with normal business hours or off-hours maintenance?
5. **Administrative / Routine vs Hostile**: Could this be legitimate system administration, automated scripts, or scheduled backup/maintenance?
6. **Data Limitations & Uncertainty**: What baseline information is missing (e.g. historical login history, shift schedules)?
