## YOUR INVESTIGATION TASK

Analyze the evidence systematically:

1. **Observed Facts**: What specific events did we see? Reference event_id for each fact.
2. **Attack Pattern Analysis**: Do the events form a coherent attack progression? How do they connect?
3. **Hypothesis Formation**: What likely happened? Why do you believe this based on the evidence?
4. **Evidence Support**: For each hypothesis, list the event_ids that support it. Quantify confidence in that connection.
5. **Alternative Hypotheses**: Are there other plausible interpretations? If so, explain why your main hypothesis is more likely.
6. **Uncertainties**: Where is evidence weak, ambiguous, or missing? Be explicit about gaps.
7. **Threat Classification**: Based on all evidence, what type of attack is this? (e.g., AUTHENTICATION, LATERAL_MOVEMENT, EXECUTION, EXFILTRATION, PRIVILEGE_ESCALATION, RECONNAISSANCE, UNKNOWN, etc.)

## RESPONSE FORMAT

Respond with ONLY valid JSON. Do NOT include markdown, explanations, or preamble. The JSON must strictly match this structure:

{
  "incident_id": "string",
  "hypothesis": "string (1-2 sentences describing your primary conclusion about what happened)",
  "summary": "string (2-3 sentences explaining the attack narrative)",
  "reasoning": "string (Detailed step-by-step analysis of how you reached this conclusion, referencing evidence)",
  "supporting_evidence_ids": ["event_id_1", "event_id_2", ...],
  "observed_facts": ["fact 1 referencing event_id", "fact 2 referencing event_id", ...],
  "suspected_attack_type": "string (AUTHENTICATION, LATERAL_MOVEMENT, EXECUTION, EXFILTRATION, PRIVILEGE_ESCALATION, RECONNAISSANCE, UNKNOWN, etc.)",
  "uncertainty": "string (Describe what you're uncertain about, ambiguities in evidence, missing information, or gaps in the timeline)",
  "confidence": 0.75
}

CRITICAL RULES:
- supporting_evidence_ids MUST contain ONLY event_ids that actually exist in the input. Do NOT invent event IDs.
- observed_facts MUST reference the specific event_ids they come from.
- If uncertainty is high (>30% chance alternative hypothesis is correct), express this in uncertainty field.
- confidence must be 0.0-1.0 reflecting your overall confidence in the primary hypothesis.
- If there's insufficient evidence for any claim, explicitly say so in uncertainty.
- Do NOT make up details not present in the evidence.
